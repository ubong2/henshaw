About Henshaw

Henshaw Capital Partners is a Nigerian private equity and venture capital firm dedicated to developing products and services that catalyse the industry. We organise the Nigerian National Private Equity Initiative, raising awareness of private equity and venture capital finance for growing Nigerian businesses and creating jobs.

"We believe a robust private equity and venture capital industry is a key component of a broader financial industry for growing businesses and creating jobs."

Barbara James, Founder, Henshaw Capital Partners

Our Approach

How We Drive Change

Our methodology is built on collaboration, research, and a deep understanding of Nigeria's financial ecosystem. We engage stakeholders at every level to build a thriving private equity and venture capital industry.

From grassroots awareness to high-level policy advocacy, we create the conditions for capital to flow into growing businesses, creating jobs and sustainable value.
